Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information UpBee ("the App", "we", "us") collects, how it is used, and what choices you have. UpBee is designed to work primarily on your device, with an optional account for backup and syncing across devices. This policy applies to users of UpBee worldwide.

Data controller Hakan Özdemir · Ellernbuschfeld 38 · 30539 Hannover, Germany

What information we collect

UpBee stores most of your activity locally on your device by default. If you create an account, a copy of the following categories may also be securely stored in our cloud database so it can sync across your devices:

CategoryExamples
Account informationEmail address, display name, avatar, a friend code used to connect with others
Activity postsCaptions and media you choose to post — up to 10 photos or short videos per post — shared with friends, or publicly if you mark the post public
Habit & task dataHabits, to-dos, schedules, completion history
GoalsGoals you set and the habits linked to them
Diary & mood entriesPersonal journal text and mood selections — private to you, never shared with other users
Streak & progress dataStreak counts, milestones, and related progress indicators
Social & sharing dataFriend connections you approve, and details of tasks or goals you choose to share with friends
PreferencesApp theme, notification settings, and similar configuration choices

We do not access your contacts or precise location. We do not currently ask for age range or sex, including during onboarding. If we add those questions later, we will update this policy first. Any such answers would stay private to you and would not be used to choose ads.

Camera and photo library access are optional and only requested when you use them:

  • Camera — to take a photo or record a short video for an activity post
  • Photo library — to attach photos or short videos to an activity post (up to 10 per post), and, if you choose, to save a shareable win image

What is visible to other users

UpBee includes optional social features. What other people can see depends on what you choose to share:

  • Your display name, avatar, and friend code
  • Progress on tasks or goals you have explicitly chosen to share
  • Activity posts you publish, including captions and up to 10 photos or short videos, to the audience you pick (friends, or public)
  • Streak milestones, if you choose to share them

Why we process your data

We process your data on the following legal bases (Art. 6 GDPR):

  • Performance of a contract — to provide the core functionality of the App you have requested
  • Consent — for optional features such as push notifications, camera, and photo library access, which you can withdraw in your device settings, and for personalized advertising where consent is required
  • Legitimate interest — to maintain the security, stability, and reliability of the service
  • Legal obligation — where applicable, e.g. for tax and business record-keeping

You can limit ad personalization in your phone's privacy settings.

How long we keep your data

Your data is kept for as long as you maintain an account. If you delete your account, your personal data is removed from our active systems; residual copies may remain in encrypted backups for a limited period of typically up to 30 days before being permanently purged.

Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Export a copy of your data — available directly in the App via Settings → Export my data
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Withdraw consent at any time, without affecting prior processing
  • Lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority — for Lower Saxony, this is the Landesbeauftragte für den Datenschutz Niedersachsen

You can exercise most of these rights directly within the App (Settings → Account, Export, or Delete account), or by contacting us at hello@upbee.app.

International users

UpBee is available to users around the world. This policy is built around the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) as our baseline standard, because it is one of the most protective privacy frameworks available. Regardless of where you live, we apply the same core protections described in this policy — including access, correction, deletion, and export of your data.

If your country of residence provides additional or different rights under its own privacy laws, those rights apply to you as well, to the extent required by local law.

Children

UpBee is not directed at children. As a general, worldwide baseline, we ask that users under the age of 16 only use the App with the involvement and consent of a parent or legal guardian — even where local law permits a lower age of consent. If we become aware that we have collected personal data from a child without appropriate consent, we will delete it promptly.

Security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including encrypted storage and transmission, and access controls that restrict data to the account it belongs to. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example to reflect new features. Material changes will be announced within the App. Continued use of UpBee after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to hello@upbee.app.
